MONEY CASE TOOLS

Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

What this tool is

Money Case Tools is a private tool used only by the owner of The Money Case, a YouTube channel, to read statistics about his own channel. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it reads

When the channel's owner signs in with his Google account and gives permission, the tool gets read-only access to:

  • his own channel and its videos, through the YouTube Data API (read-only);
  • his own channel's statistics, such as views, watch time, audience retention and impressions, through the YouTube Analytics API (read-only).

It also reads public information about public YouTube videos and channels, such as titles and view counts, which anyone can see on YouTube.

The tool cannot upload, edit or delete videos, change any setting, or post comments.

How the information is used

The information is used only to write private reports for the channel's owner about how his own videos perform. The owner may use software assistants, including AI services, to help him read these statistics for his own use.

The information is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone for any other purpose.

Where it is kept

The sign-in permission and the reports are stored on the owner's own computer. The owner can delete them at any time, and can withdraw the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Other people's information

The tool does not collect personal information from anyone else. Nobody other than the channel's owner can sign in to it.

YouTube and Google

The tool uses YouTube API Services. Use of YouTube is covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
