Money Case Tools

Money Case Tools is a private, read-only tool used by The Money Case, a documentary channel on YouTube, to read the channel's own statistics: views, watch time, audience retention and thumbnail impressions.

It is used only by the channel's owner, to write private reports about his own channel. It is not a public service, it has no other users, and nobody else can sign in to it.

The tool can only read. It cannot upload, edit or delete videos, and it cannot post comments.
